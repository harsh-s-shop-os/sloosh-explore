Every icon, and how it moves

Hover or press any card to play its icon. The big one shows the motion up close; the small one is the size it really is on the page. On the real page, the icon plays when you hover or press the button, tab or tag it sits in.

  • When in doubt, redrawPlain icons redraw themselves. Plus and minus never rotate.
  • Only when you touch itHover, press, or a click that changes state. Nothing moves on scroll or on appear.
  • Contextual icons act it outA plug plugs in, a link joins, a bell would ring, a bulb would light.
  • Arrows travel, chevrons turnArrows nudge on hover and fly out and back on press. Chevrons that open something rotate with it.
  • Same icon, same motionEverywhere it appears. Different icons get different motions.
  • Small and quickAbout 0.4 to 0.7 seconds, with a small overshoot at the end for the playful ones.
  • One at a timeOnly the icon in the thing you hovered moves. Neighbours stay still.
  • The press belongs to the buttonThe keycap going down is the button’s job. The icon adds its own motion on top.
  • Touch screensNo hover there, so the motion plays on press.
  • Scenes keep their own timingIcons inside the animated scenes (hero, Spacelab, Agents) follow the scene, not the pointer.
